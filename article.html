Cut Down The Umbrella Tree

The Luo community in the lake Victoria basin believe that growing the umbrella tree within ones homestead may lead to death of individuals in the family.

MICHAEL OCHIENG'        

13 June 2023

They paticularly say that if the roots of the plant grow huge enough and get into contact with the house, there will be unusual deaths observed in the family.
They have now adopted the habit of cutting down the tree at its young stages when the roots of the tree have not yet neared the house.
This habit has been perpetuated over the past years in the Luo community.

Despite this the tree is also known for some benefits

1. Air Purifier - Removes indoor toxins
2. Easy Care - Low maintenance, great for beginners
3. Looks Good - Glossy leaves, adds beauty indoors/outdoors
4. Stress Relief - Calming and improves mood
5. Outdoor Shade - Tall types give light shade
